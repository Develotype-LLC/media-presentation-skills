## Creative brief

Audience: leaders evaluating a practical systems consulting approach and colleagues evaluating this skill library. Desired decision: choose one workflow to trace and improve. One message: understand the work, clarify the path, then build a usable slice with the team.

Concepts considered: (1) parts becoming a system, selected for material clarity; (2) an operations map, used as editable native diagrams where exact relationships matter; (3) an editorial field guide, selected as the overall layout. Metaphor limitation: generated objects are atmosphere, not a literal depiction of consulting delivery or an engineered mechanism.

Execution: local HTML assembly and local video generation. The planning/coding harness may be cloud-hosted, so this is not characterized as an entirely private/local AI workflow. No generated narration was requested or included. Runtime and cost figures belong to actual generation records; no estimated speed or cost is presented as measured evidence.
